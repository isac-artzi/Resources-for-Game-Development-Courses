Introduction to VR · Virtual Reality & Game Development

Interactive lecture notes

7 topics · 15 weeks · 30 sessions

Build a VR game — and understand why it works.

These notes accompany a fifteen-week course in virtual reality and game development with Unity 6 and the Meta Quest 3. Each topic is one page with a tab per class session. Concepts are defined where they appear, formulas are live, and the visualizations respond to your input. Open a topic, pick today's session, and follow along.

How the notes work

Tabs across the top of each topic are class sessions (Tuesday and Thursday). Boxes marked ▶ are interactive — drag, click, and change parameters. “Check yourself” questions give instant feedback. Nothing is sent anywhere; everything runs in your browser and works offline.

What you need

Unity 6.5 with Android Build Support, Visual Studio Code, a Meta Quest 3 in Developer Mode, and the starter projects from the course GitHub repository. In-class activities are desktop-first, then ported to the headset.

The course project

Realm of Legends: a VR adventure quest through an enchanted forest, a mountain pass, and ancient ruins. Each topic adds one system to your team's game, documented in your Game Design Document.

Topics

TOPIC 1 · WEEKS 1–2

Introduction to Virtual Reality

Immersion and presence, the XR continuum, how headsets see and track, Unity fundamentals, mock views at human scale, re-imagining experiences, and project planning.

4 sessions · 12 interactive figures
TOPIC 2 · WEEKS 3–4

VR Game Design Principles on a Variety of Platforms

Platform landscape, design principles for presence and comfort, configuring Unity for Quest 3, frame budgets, basic movement and interaction with the XR Interaction Toolkit, building and deploying a prototype.

4 sessions
TOPIC 3 · WEEKS 5–6

3D Modeling and VR Environments

Meshes, UVs, textures and PBR materials; assembling environments from ready-made assets; level of detail, occlusion culling, lightmapping; profiling and performance comparison.

4 sessions
TOPIC 4 · WEEKS 7–8

VR Interaction and User Interfaces

Diegetic and spatial UI, legibility and comfort zones, world-space canvases, ray, direct and poke interaction, inventories and dialogue, user testing and iteration.

4 sessions
TOPIC 5 · WEEKS 9–10

VR Locomotion and Movement

Vection and sensory conflict, teleportation and its arc, snap and smooth turning, smooth locomotion and vignettes, hybrid methods, comfort testing, and designing with a worldview.

4 sessions
TOPIC 6 · WEEKS 11–12

VR Audio and Sound Design

How we hear direction and distance, spatial audio and HRTFs, Unity's audio system and mixer, soundscapes for each environment, sound-only navigation, and audio user testing.

4 sessions
TOPIC 7 · WEEKS 13–15

VR Game Development Project

Integrating every system, scene management and builds, structured playtesting, analysing and prioritising feedback, accessibility and polish, and presenting the finished game.

6 sessions
